**Situation**
Our fiscal review of Mamaearth’s regional performance initially reported a raw total revenue of INR 99,860.20. Early data suggested that January was the strongest period, with collections peaking at INR 29,582.10. However, a granular audit of payment channels revealed significant volatility in logistics, particularly regarding the return rate by payment method (%). Our records show that COD returns stand at 44.4%, significantly higher than UPI at 18.9% and CARD at 14.7%.

**Complication**
The integrity of our growth narrative was challenged by data redundancies and non-recurring transactions. We identified a duplicate reconciliation delta of INR 2,501.90, which inflated the top line. Furthermore, the January surge was found to be artificial; after removing two bulk orders, the monthly revenue is INR 11,637.10. We also identified a highest-risk segment: COD orders from Tier-2 cities, which exhibit a return rate of 54.5%, threatening regional margins.

**Resolution**
By adjusting for these discrepancies, we have established a cleaned total revenue of INR 97,358.30. This correction shifts our strategic focus to the true peak month, March, which recorded a sustainable INR 20,318.90. To protect the bottom line, finance and ops heads must now implement stricter verification protocols for the 54.5% risk segment in Tier-2 cities. Moving forward, we will prioritize UPI and CARD transactions to mitigate the high return rates associated with COD and ensure long-term stability.
